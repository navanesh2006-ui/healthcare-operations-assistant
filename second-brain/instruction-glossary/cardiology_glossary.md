# Cardiology Instruction Glossary

- **ECHO (Echocardiogram)**: Ultrasound scan of the heart structure and blood pumping efficiency. [[Safety Rules]]
- **ECG / EKG (Electrocardiogram)**: Graph recording of heart electrical rhythm.
- **PCI (Percutaneous Coronary Intervention)**: Stent procedure to clear blocked heart arteries.
- **Dual Antiplatelet Therapy (DAPT)**: Combination of Aspirin and Clopidogrel to prevent stent blood clots. Crucial: Do not stop without cardiologist approval.
- **Titration**: Gradually adjusting medicine dosage based on heart rate or blood pressure measurements. Always flags for [[Safety Rules]] review.
